# Natal charts, sharing, horoscopes, transits and synastry

## Read and download your natal chart

Open **Natal chart** (`/natal-chart`) for the active person. The page shows a chart wheel, a written reading, placements, signs, degrees, houses, angles, aspects and retrograde markers. Expand or collapse reading sections as needed.

The zodiac is tropical. Houses normally use Placidus. If stable Placidus cusps cannot be computed, such as at extreme latitudes, the service falls back to whole-sign houses and reports that system. This can explain differences from another astrology service. Different input times, locations, timezone handling, house systems or zodiac systems also change results.

The chart includes the Sun, Moon, Mercury, Venus, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, Neptune, Pluto and the mean North Node. A retrograde marker describes apparent backwards motion from Earth.

Choose **Download PDF** for a natal report. The PDF is generated by the backend for the signed-in owner, not sent automatically by email. If downloading fails, check the session and membership, try again after any rate-limit wait, and contact support with the error. The server limits natal PDF requests to thirty per hour per account outside tests.

A computed chart is not a guarantee of personality, health, relationships or future events.

## Share a chart and revoke access

On **Natal chart**, choose **Share this chart** and copy the link. A chart share works without login. Anyone who receives the link can view the shared chart and reading.

The response includes the person’s name, birth date, birth time, city and country. It removes exact coordinate/offset/Julian-day metadata, but the birth information is still personal information. Share only with intended recipients.

The same link is reused while sharing remains enabled. Turn sharing off to revoke the token; the old link then stops working. Creating a new share after revocation produces a new token. Switching a profile off temporarily blocks its link, but that is not permanent revocation.

A deleted/closed account retains its profiles. Account closure does not clear profile share tokens in the reviewed code. If you want a link disabled, revoke it before account closure or ask support for help. Revoking access does not retrieve copies already saved by recipients.

## Daily, weekly and yearly horoscopes

Open **Daily horoscope** (`/daily-horoscope`), choose a zodiac sign and the available daily, weekly or yearly period. The service accepts a date and timezone and computes the sky for that period.

Horoscopes are **sun-sign readings**, not the same as the personalized natal transit comparison. Categories cover personal life, profession, emotions, health, travel and luck, with sky notes, Moon information and summary indicators. Health text is reflective entertainment, not medical guidance.

The written output is selected from authored English/German interpretation material using calculated planetary positions. It is not a fresh generative-AI answer on every request. The same sign/date/settings can therefore produce the same reading. For personal aspects to your birth chart, use **Transits** instead.

## Explore your personal transits

Open **Transits** (`/transits`) with the intended active profile. The timeline compares current planetary positions with that person’s natal planets and angles.

The implemented window is fourteen days: four days before today and nine days after today. “Today” follows the saved profile timezone. Drag/scrub the timeline or tap a marker; use **Back to today** to reset. Select an event to see transiting body, aspect, natal point, exact date, active span and interpretation.

The interface ranks influences by significance and distinguishes flowing and hard aspects, faster inner planets, slower outer planets and lunations. An event can be outside the selected day’s orb while still appearing in the wider timeline. An exact date outside the visible portion may still be used in event details.

Check the active person and birth time if transits seem wrong. Interpretations are authored text assembled from computed events, and are not guaranteed predictions.

## Compare two profiles with synastry

Open **Synastry** (`/synastry`), select two different enabled saved profiles and choose **Compare the charts**. Add a second person first if needed; additional slots may require a profile pack.

The comparison includes a bi-wheel, overall compatibility gauge, strongest cross-aspects and four interpretation sections: emotional connection, attraction, communication and long-term themes. Scores and bands are calculated from planetary contacts and mapped to authored reading text.

A score describes the application’s astrological interpretation. It does not establish whether a relationship will succeed or whether you should begin/end it. Different birth times and house calculations can change the result. Correct an entry through Birth details, subject to that profile’s lifetime correction limit.
