# Compare two profiles with synastry

Open **Synastry** (`/synastry`), select two different enabled saved profiles and choose **Compare the charts**. Add a second person first if needed; additional slots may require a profile pack.

The comparison includes a bi-wheel, overall compatibility gauge, strongest cross-aspects and four interpretation sections: emotional connection, attraction, communication and long-term themes. Scores and bands are calculated from planetary contacts and mapped to authored reading text.

A score describes the application’s astrological interpretation. It does not establish whether a relationship will succeed or whether you should begin/end it. Different birth times and house calculations can change the result. Correct an entry through Birth details, subject to that profile’s lifetime correction limit.
