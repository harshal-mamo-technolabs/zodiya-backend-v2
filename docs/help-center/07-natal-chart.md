# Read and download your natal chart

Open **Natal chart** (`/natal-chart`) for the active person. The page shows a chart wheel, a written reading, placements, signs, degrees, houses, angles, aspects and retrograde markers. Expand or collapse reading sections as needed.

The zodiac is tropical. Houses normally use Placidus. If stable Placidus cusps cannot be computed, such as at extreme latitudes, the service falls back to whole-sign houses and reports that system. This can explain differences from another astrology service. Different input times, locations, timezone handling, house systems or zodiac systems also change results.

The chart includes the Sun, Moon, Mercury, Venus, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, Neptune, Pluto and the mean North Node. A retrograde marker describes apparent backwards motion from Earth.

Choose **Download PDF** for a natal report. The PDF is generated by the backend for the signed-in owner, not sent automatically by email. If downloading fails, check the session and membership, try again after any rate-limit wait, and contact support with the error. The server limits natal PDF requests to thirty per hour per account outside tests.

A computed chart is not a guarantee of personality, health, relationships or future events.
