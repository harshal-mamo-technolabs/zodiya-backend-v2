# Daily, weekly and yearly horoscopes

Open **Daily horoscope** (`/daily-horoscope`), choose a zodiac sign and the available daily, weekly or yearly period. The service accepts a date and timezone and computes the sky for that period.

Horoscopes are **sun-sign readings**, not the same as the personalized natal transit comparison. Categories cover personal life, profession, emotions, health, travel and luck, with sky notes, Moon information and summary indicators. Health text is reflective entertainment, not medical guidance.

The written output is selected from authored English/German interpretation material using calculated planetary positions. It is not a fresh generative-AI answer on every request. The same sign/date/settings can therefore produce the same reading. For personal aspects to your birth chart, use **Transits** instead.
